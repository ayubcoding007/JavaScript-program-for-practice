What is an Operator?

In JavaScript, an operator is a symbol that performs an operation on 
one or more operands, such as variables or values, and returns a result. 
Let us take a simple expression 4 + 5 is equal to 9. Here 4 and 5 are 
called operands, and + is called the operator


JavaScript supports the following types of operators.
. arithmeticOperators
. comparisonOperators
. relationalOperators
. logicalOperators
. bitwiseOperators
. assignmentOperators
. miscellaneousOperators
